Privacy Policy

Block Sort - Cube Color · Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Block Sort - Cube Color (the "game"), an Android game published by ReallyAwesomeGamesStudio ("we", "us").

In short: the game itself does not ask for or collect any personal information. It shows advertisements provided by Google, and Google's advertising service collects some device information in order to do that.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves the following on your phone only, so that you can continue where you left off:

  • your current level and your coins,
  • your power-up charges,
  • your sound and vibration settings.

This information is not sent to us and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the game's data or uninstall the game. If you have Android's backup feature switched on, Android may include it in your own device backup.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information about you. The game has no account, no sign-in and no registration, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any files on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • the IP address, which can indicate your approximate location (such as country or city),
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language,
  • information about how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ad performance, and to detect fraud. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised or not personalised. Google processes this information as described in its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices

  • Consent in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your choice about the use of your data for ads the first time you open it. You can change this choice at any time: open the menu during a level and select "Privacy settings".
  • Advertising ID. In your phone's Android settings (under Google, then Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Game data. You can delete everything the game has stored by clearing its data in Android settings or by uninstalling it.

Permissions

The game uses internet access to load ads, and the vibration function of your phone for feedback while you play. It requests no other permissions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are handled by Google through the links above. You can also contact us with any question, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
